How to Use

A simple oiling ritual

Take an adequate quantity of Vedakesh Hair Oil.

Apply directly to the scalp.

Gently massage using fingertips for 5–10 minutes.

Spread the remaining oil through the lengths of the hair.

Leave for 1–2 hours, or overnight if comfortable.

Wash with a mild shampoo.

Recommended routine: 2–3 times per week for general hair care. Consistency matters more than quantity.
